What to enter. The percentile rank is where the student's score falls among students at the same SDQA level. A rank of 40 means the student reads as well as or better than 40 percent of those peers. The score is the words correct per minute (WCPM) that matches that rank in the table. The current score is filled in from the baseline median in Step 2. Enter the goal row yourself.
